====================================================================================================
CORRELATION-BASED SIP DIALOG CLASSIFIER IMPLEMENTATION
====================================================================================================
Implemented exactly as close as possible to the paper:
- padded dialogs
- zero-lag cross-correlation
- vector r of correlations against the benign knowledge base D = DN
- central moments of r
- decision based on M4 (4th central moment)
- threshold theta > max M4 computed on benign dialogs in D

Two variants are computed:
1) non-normalized cross-correlation
2) normalized cross-correlation

Binary interpretation on test.csv:
0 = benign
1 = anomalous
